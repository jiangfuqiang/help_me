简介

MarkdownMe（以下简称"本应用"）是一款专注于本地文件编辑的 Markdown 编辑器。我们非常重视您的隐私，本隐私政策将说明我们如何处理您的信息。

核心原则

我们不收集、不存储、不传输您的任何个人信息或文件数据。

数据处理方式

1. 本地存储

  • 所有文件和数据完全存储在您的设备本地
  • 应用不会将任何数据上传到远程服务器
  • 应用不会备份您的数据到云端

2. 网络访问

  • 本应用不请求网络权限
  • 本应用无法访问互联网
  • 所有功能资源（Mermaid、代码高亮等）均已打包在应用内部

3. 文件访问

  • 仅访问您明确选择打开的文件和文件夹
  • 使用 macOS App Sandbox 沙盒技术保护您的数据安全
  • 通过 Security-Scoped Bookmarks 记住您授权的文件夹访问权限
  • 不会访问您未授权的任何文件或目录

4. 使用数据

本应用不收集任何使用数据，包括但不限于：

  • 不收集使用统计
  • 不收集崩溃报告
  • 不收集用户行为数据
  • 不使用任何分析工具

5. 个人信息

  • 不要求注册账号
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱等个人信息
  • 不使用 Cookies 或追踪技术

App Sandbox 权限说明

本应用仅请求以下必要的系统权限：

权限 用途 说明

我们不请求以下权限：

  • ❌ 网络访问权限
  • ❌ 摄像头和麦克风
  • ❌ 联系人和日历
  • ❌ 位置信息
  • ❌ 照片库访问

Introduction

MarkdownMe ("the App") is a Markdown editor focused on local file editing. We take your privacy seriously, and this Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Core Principle

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information or file data.

Data Handling

1. Local Storage

  • All files and data are stored entirely on your local device
  • The App does not upload any data to remote servers
  • The App does not back up your data to the cloud

2. Network Access

  • The App does not request network permissions
  • The App cannot access the internet
  • All feature resources (Mermaid, code highlighting, etc.) are bundled within the app

3. File Access

  • Only accesses files and folders you explicitly choose to open
  • Uses macOS App Sandbox technology to protect your data security
  • Uses Security-Scoped Bookmarks to remember folder access permissions you've granted
  • Does not access any files or directories you haven't authorized

4. Usage Data

The App does not collect any usage data, including but not limited to:

  • No usage statistics
  • No crash reports
  • No user behavior data
  • No analytics tools

5. Personal Information

  • No account registration required
  • No collection of names, emails, or other personal information
  • No use of cookies or tracking technologies

App Sandbox Permissions

The App only requests the following necessary system permissions:

Permission Purpose Description
com.apple.security.app-sandbox App Sandbox Required by macOS App Store to protect your system
com.apple.security.files.user-selected.read-write Read/write user-selected files Only accesses files you explicitly open
com.apple.security.files.bookmarks.app-scope App-scoped bookmarks Remembers folders you've added for automatic display on next launch
com.apple.security.files.bookmarks.document-scope Document-scoped bookmarks Remembers recently opened files list

We do NOT request:

  • ❌ Network access
  • ❌ Camera and microphone
  • ❌ Contacts and calendar
  • ❌ Location information
  • ❌ Photo library access

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services or SDKs, including:

  • No advertising services
  • No analytics tools
  • No crash reporting tools
  • No social media integration

Data Security

  • Your data always stays on your device
  • The App runs isolated using macOS App Sandbox technology
  • The App cannot access other parts of your system
  • The App cannot communicate with other apps or networks

Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we don't collect any user information, the App is safe for users of all ages.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored on your local device:

  • Deleting the app completely removes all related data
  • You can manually delete files and folders at any time
  • The App does not retain any copies of your data in the cloud

Privacy Policy Changes

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will:

  • Notify you in app update notes
  • Update the effective date on this page
  • Inform you through in-app notifications (if applicable)

Your Rights

Under applicable privacy laws, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: Since data is on your device, you can access it anytime
  • Right to deletion: Delete the app or files to remove data
  • Data portability: All your files are in standard Markdown format and can be exported anytime

Compliance

This App complies with:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • Apple App Store Privacy Requirements
